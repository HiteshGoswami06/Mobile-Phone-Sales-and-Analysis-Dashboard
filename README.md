# Mobile Phone Sales and Analysis Dashboard -  README 📱📊

A comprehensive business intelligence dashboard designed to evaluate mobile phone market dynamics, brand pricing strategies, consumer hardware preferences, and product sales performance. This repository contains the documentation and theoretical layout for analyzing brand-level discounts, customer ratings, hardware specifications (RAM, battery capacity), and overall market trends.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview & Strategic Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to model and visualize the complex relationships between smartphone manufacturing brands, pricing models, promotional discount structures, and consumer purchasing behavior. By leveraging multi-dimensional filtering across popular brands (Apple, Poco, Realme, Samsung, Xiaomi), stakeholders can uncover structural insights into how hardware specifications and market positioning drive consumer satisfaction and sales volume.

---

## 📈 Analytical Framework & Core Sections

### 1. Global Filtering & Brand Controls

* **Brand Selection Slicer:** Enables dynamic filtering and comparative evaluation across major mobile manufacturers including Apple, Poco, Realme, Samsung, and Xiaomi.


* **High-Level KPI Overview:** Tracks macro performance metrics such as Average Brand Sales and Highest Total Sales to benchmark commercial success.



### 2. Promotional Strategy & Discount Analysis

* **Brands Highest Average Discount:** Evaluates promotional aggressiveness by analyzing which manufacturers offer the highest average discount percentages, highlighting pricing elasticity across brands like Poco, Samsung, Xiaomi, Realme, and Apple.


* **Discount Percentage Relation with Sales:** Explores the correlation between promotional discounting strategies and resulting sales volumes to understand price sensitivity in the mobile marketplace.



### 3. Product Performance & Top Models

* **Models with Maximum Sales:** Identifies flagship or high-performing individual device models (such as Redmi Note series and Galaxy variations) that drive the highest transaction volumes.


* **Models on the Basis of Maximum Rating:** Highlights top-tier smartphone models prioritized by user satisfaction and consumer reviews (including variants like X3 Pro, X7 5G, and X7 Max).



### 4. Consumer Hardware Preferences

* **RAM Popularity Among Customers:** Analyzes consumer demand distribution across different RAM configurations (such as 4GB, 6GB, and higher tiers) to determine memory capacity preferences.


* **Sales by Battery Capacity:** Evaluates how battery capacity specifications influence consumer purchasing trends and overall market adoption.



### 5. Cross-Brand Market Trends

* **Market Trends on Sales:** Examines comparative sales volumes across competing brands to establish market share hierarchies.


* **Market Trends on Pricing:** Evaluates pricing positioning strategies across different manufacturers.


* **Market Trends on Average Rating:** Assesses consumer sentiment and brand loyalty through comparative average satisfaction ratings.



---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Data Visualization Tool:** Power BI / DAX


* **Data Modeling:** Multi-table relational schemas mapping transactional sales figures, hardware specifications, customer review metrics, and promotional pricing data.



---

## 🚀 Usage Guide

1. Open the dashboard project file within your designated Business Intelligence workspace.
2. Utilize the interactive brand selection controls to dynamically refresh all connected visual elements and isolate specific manufacturer data.


3. Hover over individual graphical data points to inspect detailed underlying breakdowns regarding temporal sales trends, pricing distributions, and hardware correlations.
